Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Who we are

lottohelper.ca is an independent informational website. It reprints publicly posted lottery results and explains odds. It is not a lottery operator and does not sell tickets. See the contact page.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, ticket numbers or payment details. We cannot cash a ticket and we have no reason to store one.

The hosting provider and content-delivery network keep ordinary server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, approximate time). Those logs are used for uptime, abuse control and debugging. We do not sell them.

This site uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may set cookies or use similar technologies to measure or personalise ads. You can use Google Ads Settings and your browser controls to limit that.

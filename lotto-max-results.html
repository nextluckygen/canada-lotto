Lotto Max results archive

Every Lotto Max draw recorded on this site since Tuesday, July 28, 2026, with the winning numbers, bonus number and the jackpot information that was published for each draw, followed by descriptive statistics calculated from the same data.

Lotto Max is drawn every Tuesday and Friday. Since April 2026, a $6 play gives you four selections of seven numbers from 1 to 52: you choose one line (or take a Quick Pick) and the terminal adds three Quick Pick lines. Seven main numbers and a bonus number are drawn. Matching all seven on one line wins or shares the main jackpot, which starts at $10 million and is capped at $90 million. Every draw also has $100,000 MaxPlus prizes, and once the jackpot passes $50 million there are $1 million MaxMillions prizes as well. The full mechanics are in our Lotto Max and MaxMillions guide.

How this archive is built: a script reads Western Canada Lottery Corporation's public winning-number page after each draw night. A row is only added when the date, the weekday and the count of numbers all check out, so a missing row means the result could not be verified automatically, not that a draw did not happen. The jackpot column comes from the jackpot figure WCLC advertised before that draw; it was recorded starting in October 2026, so earlier rows show a dash. Always confirm a ticket against WCLC or your own provincial lottery before you rely on it.

Number frequency across 20 recorded draws

Each draw shows 7 main numbers out of 52, so in 20 draws each number would appear about 2.7 times on average. Random variation around that average is normal: roughly 95% of numbers should land between 0.0 and 5.7 appearances (two standard deviations either side). In this archive 2 of 52 numbers fall outside that band (43, 49), which is in line with chance.

Most frequent so far: 43 (6×), 49 (6×), 1 (5×), 23 (5×), 39 (5×). Least frequent: 16 (0×), 33 (0×), 7 (1×), 9 (1×), 14 (1×). 2 numbers have not appeared at all in this archive yet (16, 33); with only 20 draws that is expected — the chance a given number misses 20 draws in a row is about 5.5%. None of this changes the next draw: every number has exactly the same chance each time. See why hot and cold numbers do not predict anything.

Sums and consecutive numbers

The sum of the 7 main numbers has ranged from 138 to 269 here, averaging 201.0. Over the long run the average sum is exactly 185.5. At least one pair of consecutive numbers (such as 14 and 15) appeared in 9 of 20 draws (45.0%); the long-run probability is 60.0%, which surprises many people who avoid consecutive numbers on purpose.

What this page cannot tell you

It cannot tell you what will be drawn next. Lotto draws are certified random and independent; past frequencies, splits and sums describe what already happened and nothing more. It also cannot confirm a prize: winning-ticket locations, prize shares and claim rules are published by the lottery corporations. For that, use the official WCLC Lotto Max page, BCLC's site or app in British Columbia, or your own provincial operator, and read our guide to checking and claiming a prize.

About Lotto Max and this archive

Lotto Max is drawn every Tuesday and Friday at about 10:30 p.m. ET (7:30 p.m. PT). Since April 14, 2026, a $6 play gives you four selections of seven numbers from 1 to 52: you choose one line (or take a Quick Pick) and the terminal adds three Quick Pick lines. Seven main numbers and a bonus number are drawn. Matching all seven on one line wins or shares the main jackpot, which starts at $10 million and is capped at $90 million. Every draw also has $100,000 MaxPlus prizes, and once the jackpot passes $50 million there are $1 million MaxMillions prizes as well. The full mechanics are in our Lotto Max and MaxMillions guide.

This archive holds all 1,276 Lotto Max draws since the first one on Friday, September 25, 2009. The number range has changed twice: 503 draws used 1–49 (2009–2019), 722 used 1–50 (May 2019 – April 2026) and 51 so far use 1–52. Because a bigger range lowers every number's share, the statistics below never mix formats: the default view is the current 1–52 format, and the older eras have their own clearly labelled tabs.

How it is built: draws since October 2025 come from the Western Canada Lottery Corporation's public winning-number pages and are added automatically after each draw night, once the date, weekday and count of numbers pass validation. Older draws were added in October 2026 from Loto-Québec's public results archive and cross-checked against WCLC's draw numbering. The jackpot column shows the jackpot WCLC advertised for that draw; it was only recorded from October 2026, so earlier rows show a dash. Always confirm a ticket with WCLC or your own provincial lottery.

What this page cannot tell you

It cannot tell you which numbers will come up next. Lotto draws use certified random equipment, and each draw is independent of every draw before it. A number that has not appeared for 29 draws has exactly the same chance as the number drawn last night. Over thousands of draws, the counts drift toward the expected value, but in any short window some numbers will always look hot and others cold.

It also cannot tell you whether a ticket won. Prize amounts depend on how many people matched, and official records are the only basis for a claim. Use your ticket checker or WCLC's winning numbers to confirm, and see our guide on claiming a prize in BC and Western Canada.

What it can do: show you the complete record honestly, let you check your own line against all 1,276 draws, and put the odds in context with the odds calculator. Sources: WCLC (draws since October 2025 and all jackpot and Gold Ball data) and Loto-Québec (earlier draws).

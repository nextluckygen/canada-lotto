Why the odds did not move after this draw

A Lotto Max main jackpot line is 1 in 33,294,800 (seven numbers from 1-50). The machine does not remember last week. Drawing 2, 4, 20, 23, 25, 41, 47 tonight does not make those balls hotter or colder tomorrow. Buying two tickets doubles both the cost and the chance because you bought two combinations, not because a system unlocked the drum. Rollovers change the prize at the top of the chart. They do not change the number of ways to fill a slip. Longer notes: /odds.html.

Understanding Canadian Lottery Probabilities & Statistical Trends

1. Lotto Max: Rules, History, and Prize Structure

Lotto Max is Canada's flagship nationwide lottery, coordinated by the Interprovincial Lottery Corporation (ILC) and offered in every province through regional lottery operators. Draws are held every Tuesday and Friday evening.

2. Lotto 6/49: Rules, History, and the Gold Ball Draw

Lotto 6/49 has been running since 1982. Draws take place every Wednesday and Saturday. The Classic Draw features a fixed $5 Million jackpot, alongside the Gold Ball Draw which guarantees a $1,000,000 winner on every draw.

Play only if it still feels like a small entertainment expense

Lottery tickets are legal for adults in Canada under provincial rules. They are a poor way to pay rent, climb out of debt or “invest.” The odds page exists so that sentence is not just a slogan.

Hard lines we recommend

  • You must be the legal age in your province (19 in most of Canada; 18 in Alberta, Manitoba and Quebec — check your operator).
  • Decide a monthly cap before you see a jackpot headline. Stay under it.
  • Do not chase a loss with a larger ticket the next draw.
  • Do not borrow money to play.
  • If checking results is the first thing you do every morning and it sours the day, stop.

Help in Canada

  • Responsible Gambling Council
  • Gambling Support BC — 1-888-795-6111
  • ConnexOntario — 1-866-531-2600
  • Provincial and territorial help directory (RGC)
  • If you are in immediate distress: Crisis Services Canada — 9-8-8 or 1-833-456-4566

Self-exclusion and spending limits, where offered, are set up through your provincial operator — not through this website.

How this site tries not to make the problem worse

We do not run countdown clocks that look like a sale. We do not promise that a shuffled line is smart money. We do not email “tonight’s locked numbers.” If a future feature would only work by nagging you to play more, it will not ship.

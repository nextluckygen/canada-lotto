How the national games work — and where this site stops

Lotto 6/49 and Lotto Max are interprovincial games. You buy a ticket from a retailer licensed in your province or territory. The draw is the same across participating regions. Prize claims follow the rules of the corporation that issued the ticket.

Who is who

Depending on where you live, the face on a ticket will be one of the provincial or regional operators — BCLC in British Columbia, WCLC in the Prairies and the territories it serves, OLG in Ontario, Loto-Québec in Quebec, and the Atlantic lottery operator in the Atlantic provinces. The Interprovincial Lottery Corporation coordinates the national games. WCLC’s public winning-number pages are a convenient official feed for both 6/49 and Max, which is why this site checks those pages first.

lottohelper.ca is none of those organizations. We do not print tickets, take payment, hold a prize pool or decide a winner.

Draw nights (typical schedule)

  • Lotto 6/49: Wednesday and Saturday
  • Lotto Max: Tuesday and Friday

Holiday changes are announced by the operators. If a draw night moves, wait for the official page. This site only updates a row after the published result passes a date-and-count check.

What you are buying

A basic 6/49 play is six numbers from 1 to 49. A basic Max play is seven numbers from 1 to 50. Extra games such as Extra, Gold Ball on 6/49, MaxMillions and provincial add-ons are separate products with their own prices and prize charts. Read the back of the ticket or the official how-to-play page for the product you actually paid for.

Where to check and where to claim

Check numbers on the operator site or app for your province, or on the WCLC winning-number pages. Winning ticket locations, if published, appear in the official prize breakdown — not here.

Small prizes are often paid at retail, subject to retailer cash limits. Large prizes require a claim centre and identification. Rules differ by province and by amount. Use the claim instructions from the corporation that sold the ticket. Do not send photos of tickets to a stranger who DMs you after a draw.

Online play

Some provinces let you buy official games through the operator’s own website or app. That is not the same thing as an offshore “lottery ticket” shop. If a site is not your provincial corporation, treat it as a different product with different legal status. This website will never add a buy button.

Why jackpots on helper sites go stale

Advertised jackpots move after a win, after a rollover, and sometimes when a Gold Ball or MaxMillions layer is added. Scraping that number and showing it as a huge headline is how a lot of helper sites get both outdated and breathless. We would rather leave the live jackpot on WCLC and show the last certified combination instead.

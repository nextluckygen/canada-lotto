Results are not official

Winning numbers and jackpot figures on this site are copied from public official sources and checked automatically, but they may contain errors or be delayed. They cannot be used to claim a prize. Only the lottery corporation that sold your ticket can confirm whether it has won. Check your ticket with that corporation's app, website or a retailer ticket checker.

No prediction, no system

Lotto Max and Lotto 6/49 draws are random and each draw is independent of every previous one. Frequency tables, "hot" or "cold" numbers, odd/even splits and other statistics on this site describe past results only. They do not predict future draws and cannot improve your odds. Anyone who sells a lottery "system" or guaranteed numbers is selling something that does not exist.

Gambling involves risk

Buying a lottery ticket is paying for entertainment, not making an investment. Most tickets win nothing, and on average players get back less than they spend. Only play with money you can afford to lose, and only if you are of legal age in your province or territory (19 in most of Canada; 18 in Alberta, Manitoba and Quebec). This site does not sell tickets or accept bets.

If gambling is no longer fun, free and confidential help is available 24/7. In British Columbia call Gambling Support BC at 1-888-795-6111. In Ontario call ConnexOntario at 1-866-531-2600. If you are in crisis, call or text 9-8-8. More resources: responsible play.

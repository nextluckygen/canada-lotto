Lotto 6/49 results archive

Every Lotto 6/49 draw recorded on this site since Wednesday, July 29, 2026: the Classic Draw numbers, the bonus number and what happened in the Gold Ball draw, followed by descriptive statistics calculated from the same data.

Lotto 6/49 is drawn every Wednesday and Saturday. A $3 play has two parts. In the Classic Draw you pick six numbers from 1 to 49; matching all six wins or shares a fixed $5 million jackpot, and smaller prizes start at two numbers plus the bonus. Each play also carries a 10-digit Gold Ball number. One Gold Ball number is drawn every time, so somebody always wins either the guaranteed $1 million (white ball) or the Gold Ball jackpot (gold ball), which starts at $10 million and grows by $2 million after every white ball. Our Gold Ball guide explains the drum and the odds.

How this archive is built: a script reads Western Canada Lottery Corporation's public winning-number page after each draw night and adds a row only when the date, weekday and numbers pass validation. The Gold Ball jackpot column is calculated from WCLC's published rule and the published results (for example, the gold ball drawn on September 19, 2026 paid $32,000,000, which fixes the value of the eleven white-ball draws before it). Rows where it cannot be worked out show a dash. Confirm any ticket with WCLC or your provincial lottery.

Number frequency across 20 recorded draws

Each draw shows 6 main numbers out of 49, so in 20 draws each number would appear about 2.4 times on average. Random variation around that average is normal: roughly 95% of numbers should land between 0.0 and 5.4 appearances (two standard deviations either side). In this archive 1 of 49 numbers fall outside that band (45), which is in line with chance.

Most frequent so far: 45 (6×), 1 (4×), 6 (4×), 13 (4×), 17 (4×). Least frequent: 29 (0×), 40 (0×), 2 (1×), 15 (1×), 16 (1×). 2 numbers have not appeared at all in this archive yet (29, 40); with only 20 draws that is expected — the chance a given number misses 20 draws in a row is about 7.3%. None of this changes the next draw: every number has exactly the same chance each time. See why hot and cold numbers do not predict anything.

Balanced splits are common simply because there are many more ways to make them, not because the drum prefers them. An all-odd ticket and a three-odd ticket are equally likely to win if they are specific combinations; the "balanced" category just contains more combinations.

Sums and consecutive numbers

The sum of the 6 main numbers has ranged from 79 to 197 here, averaging 144.7. Over the long run the average sum is exactly 150.0. At least one pair of consecutive numbers (such as 14 and 15) appeared in 6 of 20 draws (30.0%); the long-run probability is 49.5%, which surprises many people who avoid consecutive numbers on purpose.

Gold Ball draw tracker

For the draw on Wednesday, October 7, 2026, WCLC lists a Gold Ball jackpot of $18 million with 26 balls in the drum, so the chance that the gold ball comes out that night is 1 in 26.

Gold Ball wins recorded in this archive: Saturday, September 19, 2026 ($32 million).

After each gold-ball win the drum is reset to 30 balls (29 white, 1 gold) and the jackpot to $10 million. White balls are not put back, so the gold ball must appear within 30 draws, and the largest possible Gold Ball jackpot under the current rule is $68 million (the 30th draw of a cycle). A useful consequence: looking forward from a reset, the gold ball is equally likely to be drawn in any of the 30 draws of the cycle (1 in 30 each), so the average jackpot when it is finally won works out to $39 million. The odds that your ticket wins either the $1 million or the jackpot are one in the number of Gold Ball selections sold for that draw.

What this page cannot tell you

It cannot tell you what will be drawn next. Lotto draws are certified random and independent; past frequencies, splits and sums describe what already happened and nothing more. It also cannot confirm a prize: winning-ticket locations, prize shares and claim rules are published by the lottery corporations. For that, use the official WCLC Lotto 6/49 page, BCLC's site or app in British Columbia, or your own provincial operator, and read our guide to checking and claiming a prize.

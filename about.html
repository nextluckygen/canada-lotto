About lottohelper.ca

This is an independent, unofficial reference. It reprints publicly posted Lotto Max and Lotto 6/49 numbers and explains the odds in ordinary language. It is not a lottery corporation, not a ticket shop, and not a prediction desk.

How the nightly job works

  1. After midnight Pacific time on a draw night, a script fetches the public WCLC result pages.
  2. Date, main numbers and bonus must parse cleanly and match the expected weekday for that game.
  3. Jackpot tickers are read only when the homepage block looks complete. If Gold Ball “balls remaining” is an unexpected number, we still keep the certified combination and send you to WCLC for the live jackpot — we do not invent a dollar figure.
  4. Failed checks leave yesterday’s verified row on screen. Guessing is not allowed.
  5. Verified draws accumulate in draw_history.json and feed the frequency grid. That grid is a description of a short sample, not a forecast.

What changed for content quality

We no longer create a new “Official Draw Results & Analysis” page after every draw. Those pages repeated the same five headings with new digits. That pattern is thin content. New results now update the homepage and the history file. Longer explainers live on separate pages that do not expire every 48 hours.

What the numbers mean

Every draw is independent. Hot/cold counts, odd/even splits and the shuffle button do not change 1-in-13,983,816 (6/49) or 1-in-33,294,800 (Max). See Odds explained and Hot and cold.

What we are not

Not affiliated with ILC, BCLC, WCLC, OLG, Loto-Québec or any other Canadian lottery operator. Game names belong to their owners and are used only to identify public draws.

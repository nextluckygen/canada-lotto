About lottohelper.ca & Analysis Methodology

Our Analytical Mission

lottohelper.ca is an independent statistical intelligence portal engineered for Canadian lottery enthusiasts. Our system automatically aggregates, filters, and analyzes historical drawing outcomes for Canada's major nationwide lottery games: Lotto Max and Lotto 6/49.

Mathematical Framework & AI Modeling

While every lottery draw is strictly an independent probabilistic event, historical pattern analysis provides valuable mathematical insight. Our statistical engines evaluate:

  • Moving Average Frequency: Measuring draw frequencies over a rolling 180-day cycle to identify hot, mid, and cold clusters.
  • Even/Odd Parity Distribution: Combinatorial filtering to maintain historically optimal ratio distributions across generated lines.
  • Consecutive Number Spans: Analyzing number clustering tendencies to generate balanced statistical distributions.
